Athlete FAQ & Tracking

Is BMI accurate for athletes or bodybuilders?

BMI does not distinguish between muscle mass and fat tissue. Highly muscular individuals may score in higher ranges despite having low body fat percentages.

How often should I track my BMI metrics?

Tracking once every 2-4 weeks provides a reliable view of trends without hyper-focusing on daily fluid weight changes.
